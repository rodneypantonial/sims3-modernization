# Live-mode fallback for unseen Sims ("hitch guard")

2026-10-26. Rodney approved: in **live mode only**, no freeze >= 300 ms may come from routing work for a Sim the player cannot see; target cap ~100-150 ms per event. Remaining hitches should come only from Sims the player can see ("then it is understandable the PC slows down"). Build/Buy, CAS, Edit Town, loading, saving and travel transitions are out of scope.

Design + build of phases B and A (ts3native 0.36.89, ts3fixes switches `unseenfbstuck` / `unseenfbplan` / `unseenfbteleport`, all **off**), selftests only, **no game run**. Ledger §366. Counters: ts3fixes 303-312, ts3native v138 `uf*`. Queue `tools/harness/queues/unseen-fallback.json` (dry-run only). **Phases C1-C4 built 2026-10-26** (§3.4.1: ts3native 0.36.90 `[UnseenFallback] Sites`, counters v139 `ufc*`, ts3fixes `unseenfbsites` 323-325, all off, selftest only, no game run; ledger §367; queue `tools/harness/queues/unseen-fallback-c.json`).

## 1. What the hitches are (inputs)

From docs/196 (after P6, release defaults) [measured there]:

| World | Cause | >= 300 ms per 10 min | >= 1 s | Unseen share known? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SV | stuck-check probe plans, sync on the sim (`StuckCheckDrain`, Foreign, `nojob`) | 4.2 | 2.4 (all SV >= 1 s) | no; the 3 AM check runs for every actor that did not move (townies asleep across town) [inferred: mostly unseen] |
| IP + SV | sim waits behind the running route job (`+0x918`, gates, destroy) | IP 6.0, SV 3.1 | 0 | no; the wait lasts as long as the rest of the job (SV 464 ms wait behind a 486 ms job) [measured] |
| IP | sync `ReplanFromPosition` / `Replan` / `Route_Plan` on the sim | 3.4 | 1 | no |
| IP, SV | locomotion stage, no wait key | IP 1.7, SV 1.4 | 0 | no |

None of these runs split the cause by "seen / unseen" Sim. `[UnseenProbe]` (docs/143 §5, built, queue `unseen-split.json` not run) can: §5 below.

**Key point for the design [inferred from docs/196 §3 #2 and docs/197 §1]:** a wait behind a route job is caused by that job's **follower**, not by the Sim that waits. So "routing work for an unseen Sim" = (a) a sync plan whose route follower is unseen, (b) a route job whose follower is unseen (every sim-thread `+0x918` taker waits for it), (c) the stuck check's throwaway plans for an unseen Sim. One per-route deadline on (a) and (b) caps every wait site (steering, avoidance, slots, placement, gates) behind an unseen Sim's job at once, without touching the sites. The site-level fallbacks (§3.3) are then only for the rest: a visible waiter blocked by... nothing unseen (allowed by the rule), or an unseen waiter blocked by a visible Sim's job (allowed: the cause is visible).

### 2.1 Camera checks (built, ts3native `UfCamMsg`)

The camera is read where `[UnseenProbe]` reads it (docs/143 §5, verified there): `Camera_GetPosition 0x73D410`, `Camera_GetTarget 0x73D480`, the frustum of `0x96B850` and its sphere test `0x68D130` (`UnRefreshCamera`), on the sim thread (ts3fixes asks from script calls, which run there), cached 30 ms. EA's per-agent on-screen byte (`agent+0xC8`, `0x954240` -> `0x980E00`, 3x the model bounds) uses the same frustum; a query passes a radius instead (3 m for a Sim or a teleport goal, the bounding circle for a lot). Query over the 0x83 channel: codes 3/4/5 = centre x/y/z, 6 = radius, answer 1 = unseen for sure. "Seen" is the default for every unknown (no camera, incomplete query, older ts3native: nothing acts).

| Case | Answer |
|---|---|
| sphere in the frustum | seen |
| sphere's nearest point inside `max(80 m, 2 |cam y - target y|)` of the camera position or target | seen |
| wide / zoomed-out camera: reach >= 240 m (camera 120 m+ above its target) | everything seen |
| map view (`CameraController.IsMapViewModeEnabled`, checked in ts3fixes) | everything seen |
| camera lot, active household lot, or a lot whose bounding circle is in view | Sims on it seen (rule 3) |

Teleport goal (§3.2): the goal point (3 m) and its lot (`GetLotAtPoint(goal)`: not the camera's lot, circle not in view) must both be unseen, checked again right before the move together with the Sim's own rule (same tick). D / D-arrive / D-leave re-run both checks at every hold wake-up and right before their move (§3.5-§3.6), and test hand-off points the same way.

Rate limit per Sim (§3.2): after a cut plan that was not recovered by a teleport, the same Sim's plans to the same 1 m destination cell fail at once for 1.5 s real time (no search); a Sim already cut 4 times in 10 s is left alone for the rest of that window (its plans run uncut, as without the switch) so a route the Sim must take is never failed forever.

Shortcut [inferred]: rule 7 reads only the current interaction's target, not linked or queued interactions; a crowd social with a visible Sim through a third Sim is not seen.

## 3. Sites

### 3.1 A: sync route plans and route jobs of unseen Sims (built, phase A)

**Owner Sim:** the route's follower (`Route.Follower`, the same object ts3native reads at route `+0x40/+0x44` [verified, docs/143 §5]). Every script plan funnels through ts3fixes' four `RouteBackoffHook` hooks (`Route.Plan`, `Replan`, `PlanFromPoint`, `ReplanFromPoint`; `ReplanFromPoint` is the script side of `IC_Route_ReplanFromPosition`) [verified, RouteBackoff.cs + decompiled ScriptCore.Route].

**Mark.** Before the plan, when `unseenfbplan` is on and the follower passes §2, ts3fixes sends `GetOption2` with `optHi` top byte `0x83` (bits 63 + 57 + 56, no RouteOption2 name), `optLo` 1 = MARK this route. ts3native (`unseenfb.cpp` `UfMsg`) remembers the handle (sim thread only) and answers 1 when `[UnseenFallback] Enabled=1`, `ufMode` 1 and the deadline exits are installed; else 0 (older ts3native and the game answer 0: the unnamed bit is false). The mark is used by the next of:

- **an async START** of that route (own, owner-task, foreign or A10 kind 1-3): the job gets `ufUs` from its start (the smaller of `ufUs` and any deadline it already had: KindDeadlines, FbJobCapMs, probe), through the exits PlanDeadlineMs already uses on the worker (world A* cost stubs, cell A* and water entry stubs; `g_pdWkTid`) [verified exits, docs/164 §7, docs/178 T1]. The sim thread therefore waits at most ~`ufUs` + the overrun behind an unseen Sim's job, at every `+0x918` site.
- **a sync IC** of that route on the sim (`SyncIc`: NoStart, Foreign, QueueFull, M1*, NoYield...): a deadline session with `ufUs` (or the smaller existing one), also for own-task plans that `PlanDeadlineOwnMs` 0 exempts. A stuck-check last round (`t_pdScH`) stays exempt (phase B keeps unseen stuck checks from planning at all).

Every exit answers "no path" the way the game's own no-path exit does, so the plan ends as an ordinary failed plan and the script caller runs its own failure branch [verified for these exits: docs/164 §7.2, `bp-plandeadline-ab` PASS, `stuckcheck-cap-verify` PASS]. Nothing new is written in game state; crash-safety is the existing exits'.

**Cut answer.** After the plan returns, ts3fixes asks `optLo` 2 = CUT?: ts3native answers 1 once when that route's last marked plan or job hit its deadline (set at session close on the sim, or at collect from the job's `capped` flag), and clears the mark either way, so a mark never outlives its plan.

**Not marked:** plans inside a stuck check (`sStuckIn`), our own route checks (`sInOwnCheck`: SafeSpot verdicts), `Sim.IsPointInLotSafelyRoutable` (its "false" would send the game to its safe-location logic), non-Sim followers (cars, boats: their driver Sim's rule applies to the driver's plans only), pets and horses.

Default `DeadlineMs` **300** (Rodney 2026-10-26, replacing the first build's 120), a player setting clamped to [175, 300] ms (175 = the lowest a player may set) at load and on every use (also when a test writes `unseenfbus` at runtime; a clamped ini value logs `unseenfb: DeadlineMs=<v> (clamped from <raw>)` once; docs/PLAYERS.md): since 0.36.94 the setting is **strict** (Rodney 2026-10-26): the whole marked plan or job, its pre-search `+0x918` wait included (native `longest`), stays under it; the cut fires earlier by the learned overrun (§3.1.1). The queue tests both (300 = release default, one job at 175), and the player picks the trade-off (lower = more cut routes).

### 3.1.1 Strict DeadlineMs (built 0.36.94, `unseenfb.cpp` / `unseenfbc.cpp` `UfLockGate`, not run)

Before 0.36.94 the cut fired at the setting and the plan returned after it (docs/197: probe jobs cut at 300 ended at 303-395 ms; docs/164 §7: sync plans late max 30 ms [measured]), and a marked sync plan's wait for a running job's `+0x918` before its search was not cut at all (§5.2). Now:

1. **Cut earlier by the learned overrun.** Effective cut point = setting - overrun budget. Per path (context sync plan / route job; exit world A\* cost stubs / cell A\* / water) the budget is the p99 of the measured time from the deadline to the plan's return, learned from every marked plan or job one of those exits cut (4 ms histogram, whole session; start value 80 ms until 20 samples, or the largest sample if bigger; floor 30 ms). A context uses the largest of its exits' budgets; the cut point never goes below setting / 2 (300 -> >= 150 ms, 175 -> >= 87.5 ms). The minute line shows it: `unseen fallback: mode 1 deadline 300 ms, cut at <sync> ms sync / <job> ms job (overrun p99 ... world s/j cell s/j water s/j, n samples); ... lock waits n, lock-wait fails n, ... longest n ms, >= setting n`. Work past the cut that no exit stops (portal A\*, `LotGraph_Build`, goal step, script callbacks, docs/164 §7.1) is exactly what the learned overrun absorbs; a plan whose uncut part alone is longer than the setting can still pass it (`ufLong`, now >= the setting).
2. **Pre-search lock wait.** Site: `Route_PlanLocked 0x82C6E0` takes `Lock_Acquire(world+0x918, &INFINITE [0x1021CF0])` before its search, and our detour (`RunPlan`) first waits for a running job [verified: docs/11 addendum 2026-10-12, `RunPlan`]. In a marked sync session `UfLockGate` instead takes `+0x918` first through `Lock_Acquire 0x4E16F0`'s deadline form (absolute `GetTickCount`, the C1-C4 timed take, so the pump and `g_rgOffSimOpen` still apply) with the session's own deadline (= the effective cut point; the search floor is the learned overrun). Taken: the plan runs as the game's under that hold (the game's acquire inside is recursive, no queued job can start meanwhile). Timed out: `RunPlan` returns 0 before the lock = the planner's own "Failed, no blocker" path (`Route_PlanCommon` default result at `0x76156D`, no native retries, no C# fail-path plans; docs/11 Q5 [verified]; the same answer the negative cache gave in game, docs/15), counted as a cut, so the CUT? answer starts the §3.2 recovery. Nothing of the search ran and nothing is written (crash-safety as C1-C4: the block is skipped whole). Not gated: no job busy, another world, a managed call for the worker (`g_inInvoke`), `[RouteWorker]` mode 1.
3. **Counters** (in the v139 reserve, no layout change): `ufEffCutUs[2]` +0x5424 (sync, job), `ufOverP99Us[2]` +0x542C, `ufLockWaits` +0x5434, `ufLockFail` +0x5438, `ufOverSamples` +0x543C.
4. **Selftest** `TestUfStrict` (`unseenfbc-test.h`): start value, p99 by bucket, largest exit per context, few-sample rule, floor 30, one outlier in 100 ignored, setting / 2 clamp at 300 and 175, `UfSyncEnd` / `UfJobEnd` feeding it, lock gate (not marked / other world / no job: untouched; taken: finite deadline ~100 ms ahead; timed out: `RunPlan` 0, no release, CUT? answers 1; deadline past: a try).

### 3.2 Recovery (built, phase A)

On a cut answer for an unseen Sim:

1. **Teleport (switch `unseenfbteleport`, off even when `unseenfbplan` is on):** only a `Route.Plan` (kind 0) from the Sim's **own** task (its interaction will follow the route), the Sim still passes §2 plus the trip shortcuts' `TownieUnseen` (standing posture, no tomb room), not a pet, the destination (`GetApproximateOriginalDestination`) valid, >= 5 m away, unseen with its lot by §2.1 (point and lot frustum + reach, not the camera's lot), and `route.IsPointRoutable(dest)` true (the game's own point test: an object position inside a footprint answers false, so object destinations are skipped) [inferred: `Route_IsPointRoutable` answers for the route's own profile]. Then `PlaceSim` (as the trip shortcuts: dynamic footprint off, position, forward, `MovedSims` entry, one `ShortcutLog` line) and the plan runs once more, marked again, from the destination: a short plan the interaction follows. Counters `unseenfbteleport_shortcuts` / `_no_spot` (names `verify-shortcut.ts` reads).
2. **Else no-retry window:** the cut result is recorded per (Sim, destination 1 m cell); the same plan inside 1.5 s returns that failure at once (`FailFast`, all result fields, as `failrepeat`) [verified pattern, RouteBackoff.cs]. The game's own failure handling ends or retries the interaction later; no interaction is cancelled by us (the game's route-fail branch does that; a forced cancel was dropped: it would add a second path that can fire mid-animation).
3. **Held objects:** a Sim carrying anything is excluded by rule 6 and by `TownieUnseen`'s standing test, so no recovery ever moves a held object.
4. **Logging:** one `ShortcutLog` line per teleport; per cut the counters (no per-cut line: SV's 3 AM can cut dozens a minute; ts3native logs the first 20 cut lines a minute as `route deadline: ...` / long-job lines, as today). Possible stuck Sims stay log-only (`docs/data/stuck-log.md`).

### 3.3 B: stuck-check probe plans (built, phase B)

**Owner Sim:** the `RunStuckCheckHelper` argument (the Sim being checked) [verified, decompiled]. Switch `unseenfbstuck` (off): in `StuckCheckRun` (alarm or our drain task), when the Sim passes §2 and the helper's fields are readable, the check plans nothing and **re-arms itself at the same round** through the game's private `CreateAlarm(sim, mAttempt)` (new helper with the current position, 20-60 sim-minutes later) [verified, decompiled `CreateAlarm`]. A Sim that moves meanwhile ends the check through the game's own position test; a Sim that becomes seen gets the game's normal check (and its reset) at the next alarm. An unseen Sim is never reset by the stuck check while unseen: the player cannot see it standing; Overwatch and our watchdog (if installed / on) still cover a Sim frozen a sim-day. Counters 303 checks deferred, 304 checks run because the Sim was seen.

Crash-safety: the hook returns before the game's body, the same way `stuckcheckbusy` does; re-arm uses the game's own method and alarm.

### 3.4 C1-C4: site-level fallbacks for unseen **waiters** (built 2026-10-26: §3.4.1; the table is the design)

**Static verdict on the lock [verified, docs/18 §246 + our `LockAcquireHook` signature]:** `Lock_Acquire 0x4E16F0` is `fastcall(lock; const DWORD* deadline)` with an absolute `GetTickCount` deadline (`0xFFFFFFFF` = infinite) and returns `-2` on timeout; `FbApplyPending` already uses deadline 0 as a try-lock. So a **timed acquire exists natively**; the game's `+0x918` call sites all pass infinite and have no failure branch, so a timeout must be turned into an answer **at the caller**, skipping the whole locked block. With §3.1 in place these sites wait behind unseen Sims' jobs for at most ~`ufUs`; C1-C4 matter only for waits behind visible Sims' jobs, which the rule allows. So they stay designs until §5 shows an unseen-waiter share worth it.

#### 3.4.1 As built (ts3native 0.36.90, `unseenfbc.cpp`; ts3fixes `UnseenFallbackSites.cs`; built ahead of the §5 gate on Rodney's request, all off)

**Mechanism.** At each site, when a route job is busy (`AsyncBusy`), the thread is the job owner (sim), it does not hold `+0x918` (recursive or lent), serves no job managed call and has no gate frame open, and the owner Sim is unseen (below), ts3native calls the game's `Lock_Acquire 0x4E16F0` itself with a deadline `GetTickCount() + SiteWaitMs` (default 100 ms, 0-150; through `[RouteGate]`'s `LockAcquireHook`, which pumps the job's managed calls while it waits and returns -2 at the deadline [verified: the finite-deadline case in selftest]). **Taken:** the whole locked block runs as the game's under that hold (its own `+0x918` acquires inside are recursive and never wait) and the hold is released once after it. **Timed out:** the whole block is skipped with its harmless answer; nothing of it runs. **One wait per job:** after a timeout every later ask during that same job (route handle) is a try (deadline 0), and a C3 / C4 Sim answered with a fake < 1.5 s ago gets a try too (`ufcRecover`), so the sim never waits `SiteWaitMs` per agent or per retry. The waits keep their probe-P0 keys (`0x85FE10`, `0x846D21`, `0x83156D`, `0x631F40`) through `t_waitKey`.

**Who is unseen.** ts3fixes (`unseenfbsites`) pushes the ids of every Sim that passes §2 (`UfWhy == 0`; pets and horses never) every 5 sim ticks over the `0x83` channel (codes 7 BEGIN, 8 / 9 id halves, 10 END with the count); out of live mode the list is empty; ts3native treats a list older than 1.5 s as none (nothing acts), so leaving live mode or a stopped push turns the sites off within 1.5 s [inferred: no sim ticks in Build/Buy; job `sv-ufc-buildbuy` checks it]. At the wait ts3native re-checks the camera itself (unseen.cpp class 1: frustum + reach rule), so a Sim that came into view since the last push acts as seen. Owner per site:

- C1 / C2: the agent whose update runs (`t_unAgent`, from `[UnseenProbe]`'s agent call site `0x83CF52`, installed by Sites when neither the probe nor the throttle did): its own part (not a linked agent), class unseen, EA's on-screen byte `+0xC8` off, a walking Sim (state 1, Sim kind), not a rider, no group link (the `[UnseenThrottle]` holds), and its owner object's id on the list.
- C3 / C4: **the running script task**, read from MonoScriptHost `+0xBD0` (docs/119 §11.2): a Sim's own task id is its ObjectId (ts3fixes tests `Simulator.CurrentTask == sim.ObjectId` for "own task") [verified]. **Correction of the design table above:** neither IC carries the Sim. `IsRouteToObjectUnobstructed(scriptHandle, point, options, ignore, agsFlags)` has no Sim parameter, and `0x831530`'s second argument is a stand-in the IC builds from the age/gender flags (`0x74E075`), not a Sim object [verified disassembly `0x74E000..0x74E0E2` + decompiled ScriptCore `RouteManager`]; `0x831530`'s NULL test is on its third argument (the target object, `ebx`), not a Sim (docs/140 §2 says "sim"). Alarm tasks, one-shot function tasks and other objects' tasks are never on the list, so they never act.

### 3.5 D: pre-emptive teleport (Rodney's idea; built 2026-10-26 as `unseenfbpre`, off, not run: §3.5.1)

When an unseen Sim (§2) in live mode starts a route whose goal is on a lot and also unseen, plan nothing: wait the time the walk would take, then place the Sim at the route's goal and let the interaction go on. Unlike §3.2 (recovery after a cut plan) this removes the plan itself, so it removes route jobs too.

**(1) Reachability without a search.** The game has no exact O(1) "can this Sim walk there" answer [verified, docs/142 §3.2-§3.4]:

| Data | What it proves | Exact? |
|---|---|---|
| Car components of R (our union-find, docs/142 §3.1) | car-only (mode 2) reachability | exact for mode 2 only; pedestrians use modes 1/3 |
| The game's flood colours (`RTData_BuildComponents 0x66CE10`, modes 1-3, no water/boat flags) | **different colours = no path** | one-sided: the flood follows every record reference regardless of edge byte, blocked flag or mode, and **portals and doors are lot-side, not in R** [verified, docs/142 §3.2 + §3.4 table, docs/99 §1.8]. Same colour does not prove a path: walls, blocked lot edges, doors, locks and room interiors are invisible to it |

So D needs a second, lot-side proof for "same colour": **the SafeSpot verdict cache** (`SpotVerdicts`, Fixes.cs: the game's own `IsPointInLotSafelyRoutable`, a full plan from the 0.5 m goal cell to the lot's corners and edge midpoints, cached one sim-day per lot and exact route profile) [verified, Fixes.cs]. A cached "routable" verdict for the goal cell = the goal is reachable from the lot edge for that route profile; the colour test covers start-to-lot-edge (the world part). The first ask of a cell costs one plan (paid once a sim-day, budgeted as SafeSpot already is); later asks are a dictionary lookup.

Door and lot rules the verdict may not cover, so D refuses them outright [inferred until read in the decompile]:
- **locked doors**: door lock settings are per household / per Sim (`Door` lock type) [inferred], and the verdict's route profile key holds no household; D refuses any goal lot that has a door with a lock set (per-lot scan cached per load + build/buy epoch, docs/76 events), and any goal in a room behind a door (goal cell indoors: `World.GetRoomId` != 0) unless the lot is a community lot with no locked door.
- **privacy / visitor rules**: other households' homes (residential lot, not the Sim's `LotHome`) are refused (the game's visitor rules: greeted / not greeted, privacy situations); only community lots and the Sim's own home lot qualify. Apartments, shells, houseboats, resorts, diving lots and tombs refused (as the trip shortcuts already do).
- **per-Sim portal permissions** (elevators, stairs a toddler or pet cannot use, spa / private rooms of venues): refused by restricting D to adult/teen/YA/elder human Sims (`SimDescription` age, not pet) and outdoor or ground-level goals [inferred].

Unreachable (different colours, a cached "not routable" verdict) = **fail fast**: the route fails at once the way a no-path plan does (`FailFast`), the interaction's own failure branch runs; never a teleport. Unknown (no verdict and the budget spent) = plan normally.

**(2) Timing realism.** Before the move the Sim is held where it stands for the estimated walk time: straight-line distance x a detour factor (1.3) / walk speed (the Sim's walk style speed; [inferred] ~1.5-2 m/s for Walk/FastWalk, read from the route's walk style), converted to sim ticks and slept in the Sim's own task (`Simulator.Sleep` in the interaction's task: a yielding context by construction, as the async plan's `AsyncWait`). The Sim stays standing, visible to the game as "routing" (its interaction is running, so autonomy, schedules and lateness see the time pass as for a walk). Hidden (`SetHiddenFlags`) is not used: unhidden Sims keep their footprint and nobody else's plan routes through a hidden one [inferred]. A hold longer than 2 sim-hours (cross-town on foot) is capped: such trips already go through the trip shortcuts (car / lot trips).

**(3) Camera checks.** At the start (Sim and goal both pass §2 / `FarFromViewers(goal)` / not on screen) and at every wake-up of the hold (each sim-minute) and right before the move. The camera or a household Sim coming near either end cancels the shortcut: the Sim plans normally from where it stands (it lost only the hold time, which a walk would also have used) [inferred: equal or earlier arrival].

**(4) Where to hook.** Not `Route.Plan` (too low: the route object has no interaction, and Plan's caller follows the path right after) but the shared follow funnel `SimRoutingComponent.DoRoute(Route)` [verified as the shared helper, docs/141 §2: 27 direct callers reach it, every interaction `Run` route helper (`RouteToSlot`, `RouteToObjectRadialRange`, `RouteToPoint`, `PlanToLotEx`) goes through it]. NRaas GoHere replaces `DoRoute` (`SimRoutingComponentEx`, docs/184): D must stand down when GoHere's `DoRoute` is in the chain (MVID / type check), as Overwatch markers already do.

**(5) Side effects and exclusions.**
- Walk-by socials and encounters (greetings, "walk by" situations, paparazzi, ghosts, pet chases) do not happen off-screen; accepted for unseen pairs only (both ends unseen = nobody to see them) [inferred low impact]; they still happen for Sims on screen.

**(6) Expected gain [inferred].** Removes the plan and the route job of every eligible unseen route, not just the long ones: fewer async jobs means fewer `+0x918` holds, so **visible** Sims' steering / avoidance / placement waits shrink as well (docs/196: those waits equal the remaining job length; a job that never runs causes none). ### 3.6 D-arrive / D-leave: split a route at the view edge (built 2026-10-26 as `unseenfbarrive` / `unseenfbleave`, off, not run: §3.6.1)

| Case | Start | Goal | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-arrive | unseen (§2) | in view (on the active lot or near the camera) | hold for the hidden leg's walk time (§3.5 (2), distance start -> hand-off), teleport to a hand-off point H just outside the view near the goal lot, then the game plans only the short last leg H -> goal normally (the Sim walks in on screen) |
| D-leave | in view | far and unseen | plan and walk a short first leg to a hand-off point H just outside the view in the goal's direction (normal plan, short), then once the Sim passes §2 at H: hold for the remaining walk time (H -> goal) and place it at the goal (§3.5 rules), or plan normally if the goal does not qualify |

**Picking H without a long search.** Candidates, cheapest first, none needs an A\*:

## 7. Risks

- **Cut a route the Sim needed** (an unseen townie's plan that would have succeeded at 200 ms): its interaction fails; the rate limit (4 cuts in 10 s, then uncut) keeps it from failing forever; `kdAfterOk`-style reading: `unseenfb_cut` vs later success is not counted yet (open).
- **Teleport** puts a Sim at its route's destination without the walk: a door, a portal or a lot entry may be skipped. Only point-routable destinations, only own-task `Route.Plan`, off by default, verify-shortcut gate.
- **Mark crossing plans:** a nested plan of another route inside the marked plan's callbacks takes the mark slot; the outer plan then runs uncut (counted as marked, not cut) [shortcut].
