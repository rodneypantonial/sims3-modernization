## What is in

- Routefail + Go Here logging, session summary, blockers near the goal, Build/Buy counts, prior version (docs/197 §7 on master). Kept for players: lines are bounded (unseen 30 a minute, 50,000 a session as a disk guard, active household lines uncapped), the only added cost per job is one clock read and a small format, and player reports need logs alone (no questions to the player).
- `[RouteAsync] KdExemptSeen=1`: no KindDeadlines cut for a seen Sim, no `PlanDeadlineMs` or `SyncCapMs` cut for a seen or unknown Sim's sync plan. `KdSeenCeilMs=10000` is the one cut left, on jobs too. 10 s: the longest plan logged with no caps is 7,361 ms (docs/163). Worst case: ceiling plus one cell A* or water search already running; no guard above that.
- Scope covers seen non-household Sims too: it is the same `UfWhy` answer, no extra cost. A seen crowd's sync plans can now each run long. `route seen exempt` line: plans, would cut, max ms, ceiling cuts.
- GC expand streak guard (e34a9499): at streak 3 `GC_black_list_spacing` goes to 12 KB. Same behaviour; the log line drops the stack black-listed count (0x123435C has no EA row).
- No counter fields on this branch (statics): the v0.2.8 layout stays, so `kdExempt` etc. are master only and `native_set kdexemptseen` does not exist here. Switch by ini.
