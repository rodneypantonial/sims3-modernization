# v0.1.5 feature decisions: clean-night numbers (draft, 2026-10-22)

Draft for Rodney: the verdicts are **suggestions**, he decides. Filled into docs/RELEASE.md (v0.1.5 decision table).

## Method

- Data: `probe-out/profile/<queue>-<date>-<job>-*` from the queues `clean-night-0.2.0` (2026-10-21/22), `fbjobcap-ab` (2026-10-22), `clean-night-routing-extra` (2026-10-22, partial: 5 jobs), `clean-night-unseen-p2`, `destroypark-fix-verify` (partial: 1 job), and the daytime queues `defer-footprint-ab`, `destroy-park-ab`, `avoidro`, `candidate-loop-ab`, `replan-defer-ab`, `stuckcheck-async`, `overwatch-spread`, `rc-first` (all 2026-10-21). Per-job verdicts and machine-load lines: `docs/data/results/2026-10-2*-queue-*.md`.
- `--ab` jobs: per side over the counted slices (warm-up slice dropped), slices cut by `from` seconds relative to the first `seconds.csv` row. it/s = summed `iters` / seconds; hitches/min from `-hitches.jsonl` (hitch >= 50 ms); frames >= 300 / >= 500 ms counted by `wallMs`; keyed `+0x918` wait = summed `blockUs_*` deltas from `-ab-native.jsonl` / side seconds (as `tools/harness/wait-ab.ts`); route ms/s = `route` column mean; feature counters = snapshot deltas per side.
- Read rule (2026-10-22, docs/143 §8.15): single-order jobs are biased by slice position (the middle slices are slow), so **only the pair mean of an off-first and an on-first job counts** (on minus off, each job's delta averaged). Both orders are listed. Single-order and two-load runs are shown as context only.
- Load rule: "other" = other apps' share of the game CPUs from the machine-load line; >= 10% is marked **(load)** and does not count as clean.
- Measured numbers are verified data; mechanisms named in a reason are inferred unless marked.

## Open data gaps

1. `clean-night-routing-extra` has 5 of 12 jobs (partial): no clean on-first for `deferfootprint`, no clean pairs for `stuckcheckasync`, `replandefer`/`replanspread` (crowd), candidate loops.
2. `destroypark-fix-verify`: 1 of 2 jobs, 1 of 2 required clean nights.
3. `fbjobcap`: the cap never fired in an on slice of the SV pair; its cap check needs to run inside long node expansions before a rerun means anything (static finding from the route-bits log line, not yet studied in the code).
4. Spreads: no evidence the nightly alarms fired in counted slices; the queue result pages should carry the ts3fixes spread counters.
5. ErrorTrap load jobs: profiler output empty (1 row per file); only `/taskstall` lines.
6. Unseen script throttle: the three switches only measured together; SV regression needs per-switch SV pairs.
7. `SyncCapMs=1000` without `FallbackAsync` (PLAN's fallback option) cannot be expressed by `release-features.ts` (it sets `SyncCapMs` with `fallbackasync`) and has no A/B of its own.
8. Several timing jobs were at or over 10% other CPU (marked above); the routing-extra and fbjobcap queues ran 08:18-10:06 PHT, not at night.
