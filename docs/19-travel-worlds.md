# 19 Travel worlds: which destinations are worst, and what travel itself costs

Research note (2026-10-13). Question: which worlds that Sims travel to and return from are the most problematic (lag, stuck Sims, crashes, save damage on return), what the travel mechanism itself does to a save, and which 2-3 worlds we should tackle first with our harness.

Markers: **[verified]** = read in `game-copy/decompiled` or measured by us; **[community]** = player reports, linked; **[inferred]** = our reasoning, still to confirm by measurement. Community evidence for individual vacation worlds is thin and anecdotal; there is no authoritative "worst world" ranking anywhere [community, searched EA forums, Steam, MTS, 2026-10-13].

## 1. Summary

- **Travel itself** costs more than any single destination: every transition runs two full saves, creates a MiniSim for every Sim in the world you leave, keeps a separate world file (`.nhd`) per visited world in the save folder, and on return resets every Sim who is away from their home lot and sends them home in one go. All of this is verified in code (§4).
- **Worst destination by community reports: Sims University** (University Life). It has a mandatory multi-week stay, crowds of student NPCs, a known door pile-up at the Student Union and Library, a "term never ends" trap and households that vanish on enrolment.
- **Next: the World Adventures worlds.** Tombs cause routing tantrums and stuck Sims, and travel to them hangs on the loading screen. Al Simhara is the most cited of the three.
- **Oasis Landing** (Into the Future) has no world fix. The hover-train station check runs after every cross-lot plan (docs/10 V2), and the game does extra future-world work before the trip.
- **Store worlds are moves, not trips.** `MoveToNewWorld` has no return. Roaring Heights is the store world players report most for stuck Sims, and it has no fix.
- **We install none of the travel-specific fixes today.** NRaas Traveler, the ellacharmed world fixes and the University Student Union/Library shell overrides are all optional or not in the catalog (§3).

## 4. The travel mechanism (decompiled code)

All paths are under `game-copy/decompiled/Sims3GameplaySystems/`.

1. **Two full saves per transition** [verified]. `GameStates.SwitchWorlds` (`Sims3.Gameplay/GameStates.cs` ~1580) calls `LoadSaveManager.SaveTravel()` once when leaving home (StartVacation) and again when leaving the destination (EndVacation). It then loads the other world from scratch. Two saves plus one world load per leg is the "long travel loading screen" [inferred].
2. **A MiniSim for every Sim in the world you leave** [verified]. `MiniSimDescription.AddMiniSims()` (`Sims3.Gameplay.CAS/MiniSimDescription.cs:546`) runs before `Bin.ExportHouseholdForTravel` on every leg. It walks `SimDescription.GetSimDescriptionsInWorld()` and creates or updates a `MiniSimDescription` for each Sim. For each Sim that has a relationship with a traveller and no thumbnail yet, it queues a `MiniSimUpdater` task that generates a cross-world thumbnail, and it **pauses the game until all of them finish** (`Gameflow.LockGameSpeed(Pause)` ... `while (sPendingSimUpdates != 0) Simulator.Sleep(0)`). MiniSims are never removed for being old (docs/09 §1) [verified], so every trip grows `sMiniSims` by the population of both worlds, and `FindMiniRelationship` stays a linear scan [verified, docs/09]. Our opt-in SaveCleanup prunes other-world MiniSims with no links (docs/14; repro `bp-save-cleanup`, −9 minis).
3. **One extra world file per visited world**: at least 30 MB each, kept in the save folder [community, [Steam 3829787744080973906](https://steamcommunity.com/app/47890/discussions/0/3829787744080973906); docs/09]. `save-stats.ts` already sums every `.nhd` in a save folder, so it shows this.
4. **Reset storm on return** [verified]. When the household leaves the destination, `SwitchWorlds` calls `SetToResetAndSendHome()` for every Sim living in the world whose `LotHome != LotCurrent`. On top of that, `ReturnFromVacationWorld` calls `SetObjectToReset()` on every household actor. When the homeworld loads, many townies plan "go home" routes at once [inferred]; our GoHome shortcut (F9) should absorb most of them [inferred].
5. **Routable-point cache limit during travel** [verified]. On departure `Sim.sIsPointSafelyRoutable` is set with `SetLimit(5)` and cleared on `TravelComplete`. This is the same check whose stall we found in Overwatch (F18) [inferred link].
6. **Partial copies and duplicates** [community]. Tourists from other worlds are MiniSim-based partial copies; adding one to a household corrupts the save ([forums.ea.com 2059467](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-3-en/corruption-and-crashes/2059467/replies/2059471)). Corrupt outfits or family trees leave Sims missing on arrival or stuck abroad ([forums.ea.com 8251165](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-3-technical-issues-en/the-sims-3-bug/8251165/replies/8251172)). On import, the game disposes of and re-imports Sims whose descriptions clash (`ImportTravellingHousehold`, `DisposeTravelImportedSimDesc`, ~2059-2320) [verified]; failure modes there are not mapped.
7. **Crash on travel after a long session** [community], which fits address-space exhaustion (Error 12, docs/05) [inferred]. Travel loads a second world while the first world's resources are still cached. Players report that travel works right after launch and fails after about an hour of play.

## 5. Proposed goal

**Goal:** a round trip (home → destination → home) is no slower than one cold world load per leg. It produces no stuck Sims beyond vanilla, no crash in 3 consecutive round trips in one session, and save growth per trip that we can explain resource by resource (and prune where it is MiniSims).

**Tackle first:**
1. **The travel mechanism, on Bridgeport → Champs Les Sims → Bridgeport.** Champs Les Sims is the smallest destination with the fewest reports, so it isolates the cost of the travel mechanism itself. Measure the time per leg (loading screen to live mode), address space and largest free block after each leg (Error 12 margin), and `save-stats.ts Baseline-BP <after-trip>` (new `.nhd`, `TravelDB.package`, MiniSim count via `SaveCleanupCounts`). Repeat the round trip 3 times in one session to reproduce the late-session travel crash.
2. **Sims University.** Run a 10-min `profile-world.ts --tag uni` plus `stuck-scan.ts` on day 2 of a term. Then repeat with the Student Union/Library shell overrides on a copy (one change at a time, docs/07 rule) and with the ellacharmed world fix on a new game. Watch the return van ("term never ends").
3. **Al Simhara.** Run `profile-world.ts --tag egypt` and `stuck-scan.ts` in the base camp, then a tomb run with one Sim (stuck-scan only). Oasis Landing comes next, together with the docs/10 V2 station-check measurement.

**Agent actions needed** (in `mods/ts3agent/src/plugin/Handlers`; none exist today, `reflect_call` alone cannot build the dialog results):
- `travel_vacation {world: France|China|Egypt, days, sims[]}` → `GameStates.TravelToVacationWorld(world, travelerIds, days, numDaysSinceLastInDestWorld)`. This skips `TripPlannerDialog`; it uses the same entry point that `TravelUtil.TriggerTravelToVacationWorld` reaches [verified signature; that the path is equivalent is inferred, so check that the visa and trip cost are skipped harmlessly].
- `travel_university {sims[], tuition}` → `TravelUtil.TriggerTravelToUniversityWorld(List<SimDescription>, int)`.
- `travel_future {sims[]}` → the time-portal entry (to locate; `TravelUtil.CanSimTriggerTravelToFutureWorld` is the guard).
- `travel_home {sim, everyone: true}` → `TravelUtil.TriggerTravelToHomeWorld(actor, true, departing)`.
- `travel_state` → `GameStates.IsTravelling`, `DestinationTravelWorld`, `CurrentDayOfTrip`, `TripLength`, MiniSim count, `.nhd` list. The harness waits on it the way `cycle.ts` waits for live mode.
- `auto_dialog` and `respond_dialog` already exist for the travel confirmations.

### Built (2026-10-13): agent travel actions and `travel-trip.ts`

Agent commands in `mods/ts3agent/src/plugin/Handlers/TravelHandlers.cs`. They compile against the game DLLs; **none has run in game yet** [inferred until a run]. Each one returns at once, and the world switch runs as game tasks.

| Command | What it calls [verified in decompiled code] |
|---|---|
| `travel_vacation {world: france\|china\|egypt, days?: 2, sims?: [names], cost?: 0}` | Queues `TravelUtil.SitInCarToTriggerTravel` on the lead Sim (the active Sim when it travels), the same interaction `TriggerTravelToVacationWorld` queues after the trip planner. The target is the world lot, so `Run` skips the walk to the farthest lot and goes straight to `FinalBoardingCall` → `GameStates.TravelToVacationWorld`. It skips the planner's visa and duration limits and the ITF "travel with" warning. Sims that fail the game's private `CheckForReasonsToFailTravel` are left behind and listed under `skipped`, because one failing traveller makes `FinalBoardingCall` cancel the whole trip. |
| `travel_university {sims?, degree?, course_load?: 1, terms?: 1, days?, dialog?: false}` | Enrols with no dialog, doing what the enrolment dialog's accept path does (`EnrollmentDialog.ConfirmAccept` → `AcademicCareer.EnrollInAcademicCareer`): per Sim `DegreeManager.SetEnrollmentData(degree, course_load)` and the `kEnrolledInUniversity` event, `AcademicCareer.GlobalTermLength = terms`, `TravelUtil.PlayerMadeTravelRequest = true`, then `TravelUtil.TriggerTravelToUniversityWorld(sims, tuition)` (moving van, walk to the farthest lot; the van's `Run` takes the tuition from household funds). Defaults match the dialog: the active Sim alone, each Sim's last studied degree (else Business), course load 1, 1 term (van trip = terms × 7 days; `days` overrides `TravelDuration`). The enrolment runs as a game task (`async_log`); if no van gets queued it clears `PlayerMadeTravelRequest` again. Skipped versus the dialog: the SUAT reminder, the enrolment music, the "are you sure" confirmation (`AcceptCancelDialog`) and the dialog's window teardown. `dialog: true` keeps the old path: `EnrollInAcademicCareer` opens the dialog (answer with `auto_dialog`/`respond_dialog`). The mailbox survives the switch [verified in code, not yet observed during travel]. Its buffer is a static of the loader assembly, and `Mailbox.Open()` keeps an existing buffer, so the probe's cached address stays valid. Only the world flag drops between `OnWorldQuit` and `OnWorldLoadFinished`. That `OnWorldQuit` fires during a travel switch, and that the agent's poll and autopilot tasks restart in the new world as they do after a normal load, is inferred; `travel-trip.ts` also accepts `in_travel` or a changed `world` as "left".

**How to run** (a home world in live mode via `cycle.ts --save Baseline-BP` first):

```
node tools/harness/travel-trip.ts --to france --days 2 --hours 6 --trips 3 --save-stats BP-trip
```

For each leg it logs the boarding time (command until the old world unloads), the load time (until the new world is in live mode), `/memory` (committed MB, largest free block) and `travel_state` before and after, one JSON line per leg in `probe-out/travel/<to>-<time>.jsonl`. At the destination it runs `--hours` sim hours on autopilot at speed 3. `--save-stats P` saves `P-trip0` before the first trip and `P-tripN` after each return (new files; the loaded save is left alone), then runs `save-stats.ts` over them. `--to university|future` use the other commands. The repro format (`repros.json`: steps, then a stuck or counter watch in one world) cannot express a world switch, so `bp-trip-france` is this command, not a repro entry:

```
node tools/harness/cycle.ts --save Baseline-BP && node tools/harness/travel-trip.ts --to france --trips 1
```

**Repros to add** (`tools/harness/repros.json`): `travel-roundtrip-wa` (timings, save-stats, MiniSim delta), `travel-3x-session` (crash or address space), `uni-union-doors` (stuck count at the Student Union), `uni-return-van` (the van leaves on schedule), `egypt-tomb-route` (one Sim through an opened tomb path).
