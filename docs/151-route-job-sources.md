# 151. Where route jobs and sync plans come from, per second in play

Written 2026-10-22. **Static + existing data only:** no game run, no build. Question: the sim thread's routing wait is bounded by how long route work holds the routing locks (`+0x918`, QL), so fewer and shorter plans help every lock fix (P5 too). Which sources produce the plans per second in play, 5 hops up, which of them are redundant, and what would cut them. Ledger: docs/51 §221. Tags as in AGENTS.md.

Builds on docs/141 (who calls the planner, by context class), docs/04a §8 (first sampled caller table, 2026-10-13), docs/87 (the four plan ICs), docs/134 (sync fallbacks), docs/143 §8.14-8.15 (throttled Sims' replans).

## Summary

- **Every plan enters through the four script ICs** (`IC_Route_Plan 0x765160`, `IC_Route_Replan 0x765490`, `IC_Route_PlanFromPoint 0x765240`, `IC_Route_ReplanFromPosition 0x765550`), which are referenced only from the IC table; there is no native-only plan source (route follow and steering reach the planner through script: `ForceReplan`, route-action results) [verified, docs/87 §1 + docs/141 §6.1]. So the sources below are complete up to sampling error.
- **Measured volume** (seconds.csv of 60 clean-night/A-B jobs, 2026-10-21/22, 30,614 s): BP 16.1 plans/s, 46.1 ms/s sim-thread route time, 24.2 ms/s on other threads (worker jobs); IP 6.6 plans/s, **96.2 ms/s**; SV 5.6 plans/s, 20.0 ms/s [measured]. Per call in hitch iterations (60,656 hitches, `byEntry`): `ReplanFromPosition` is the dearest entry (BP 14.3 ms, IP **53.6 ms**), then `Replan` (16 / 38 ms), `PlanFromPoint` (6.9 / 39 ms), `Plan` (4.6 / 20 ms) [measured; hitch-biased upward].
- **By source** (managed stacks of 102 profiles 2026-10-18..22, 137,020 samples at 5 Hz, 16,473 inside a plan IC): plans take 10% of sim-thread wall time in BP, 16% in IP, 7% in SV [measured]. Ranked, the top four are:
  1. **Leg-end and portal replans inside `RoutingComponent.DoRoute`** (`ContinueAndPopPathAndReplan`: every boat get-out onto land, car get-out moved by FGL, portal actions flagged `mbReplan`): IP 62, BP 18 ms/s.
  2. **Houseboat gangway re-plans** (every plan or replan between a houseboat lot and another lot is thrown away and redone as two `PlanFromPoint` legs by `CheckAndUpdateForHouseboats` / FoulPlay House Boat Fix): IP 35 ms/s.
  3. **Traffic cars** (`CarNpcWithNoDriver+LiteDriver`: a world plan to a random commercial lot's parking spot per wait cycle, then a drive-off plan): BP 24 ms/s.
  4. **Our own `StuckWatchdog.CheckIdle` routability checks** (ts3fixes `watchdogidle`, `SpotVerdict` -> `IsPointInLotSafelyRoutable`): BP 8, IP 7, SV 4 ms/s.
- **Clearly redundant work** found: the velvet-rope bouncer re-routes to the slot it stands in every 5 s loop (`VelvetRopes+WatchDoor.LoopDel`, BP 9 ms/s); the houseboat fixup's discarded first plan (1 of 3 searches per houseboat route); boat leg-end replans whose remaining walk still starts where the Sim landed [inferred, counter V3 decides]; watchdog checks of Sims that planned successfully from the same spot; stand-and-wait replans every 2-3 cycles.
- **Concrete changes** (§4): ten, of which D1 (leg-end replan skip), D2 (houseboat direct legs), D3 (bouncer), D5 (watchdog) are new and cheap; expected cut of plan work (= lock hold, ~95% of plan time is the locked search: `routeSearch`/`route` 43.8/46.1 BP) **BP ~20-35 ms/s of 46, IP ~45-75 ms/s of 96** if the inferred redundant shares hold. A 5-min observe-only job (§5) measures the shares before anything is built.

### 6.1 routesrc (V1-V7, observe only)

At each of the four IC hooks (`RouteBackoffHook`) the caller is read from up to 10 `StackFrame`s above the pass-through run (route classes, our hook frames, `*Orig`, `System.Diagnostics`) and put into the §2 buckets with the Reproduce rules (`RouteSrcCore.Classify`; the §3 stacks are its self-test cases). Per bucket: plans, wall us from the IC hook (includes async waits and, for the outer plan, the nested houseboat legs), fails (V1). V2: a 64-entry ring per follower, repeat = same kind, goal within 0.25 m, < 0.5 / 2 / 10 s, split by the previous result. V3: at an S1 replan, the action `DoRoute` just ran (`GetPreviousRouteAction`: Boat, Vehicle, PortalTraversal, other Portal, GetInBoat, other) and the distance from the replan point to the popped remainder's start (< 0.5, < 2, more), plus [230] = Boat/Vehicle leg ends that pass D1's rule (counted in the action hook). V4: houseboat leg plans [231], those inside a replan's fixup (`routesrc_buckets[63]`; full plans discarded ~= legs / 2 [inferred]). V5: `LoopDel` loops with the bouncer on its slot [232]. V7: watchdog checks a success memo already answers [233]. Cost: [229] classifier us (target < 1% it/s, §5). **V8 not built:** a per-kind, own/foreign split of worker job time is ts3native work (counters v85), left to the native lane.
